FAQ

Common questions

Who can become an eBay seller with your help?

Businesses that already sell, locally, online or on other marketplaces, and want to sell globally. If you have inventory in our focus categories, you are most welcome.

What do I need to tell you to get started?

Whether you sell locally (and your store or business name), whether you sell on international websites, and which other sites you sell on. This helps us understand if you're ready to sell on eBay.

Which eBay marketplaces do you guide on?

eBay US, UK, Germany and Australia.

Do you run my store for me?

No. You own and operate your store. We give you training and guidance on best practices so you can run it successfully.

Do you need my password or account access?

No. We never ask for your password or access to your account.

I already have a Shopify store. Can I sell on eBay too?

Yes. Shopify connects to eBay easily, so your products, stock and orders can stay in sync. We guide you through it.

Are you part of eBay?

No. ArtisticVenture360 is an independent consultancy that helps businesses become eBay sellers.
