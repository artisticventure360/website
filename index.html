FAQ

Common questions

Is it really free for 90 days?

Yes. Every eBay seller who joins gets our service free for the first 90 days. After that, you decide whether to continue on a monthly plan we agree together.

Which eBay marketplaces do you work on?

eBay US, UK, Germany and Australia, for new stores and established sellers.

Can you help if my account is restricted?

Yes. We review the restriction notice, prepare the right documents and appeal, and fix the cause so it doesn't happen again. The final decision is eBay's, so we tell you honestly how strong your case looks.

What is SNAD?

SNAD means "significantly not as described". It's a return or case reason that counts against your account. We reduce it by fixing listings, photos and packaging.

Do you need my password?

No. You add us as a user in your eBay account with only the permissions we need, and you can remove us at any time.

Are you part of eBay?

No. ArtisticVenture360 is an independent company that provides services to eBay sellers.
